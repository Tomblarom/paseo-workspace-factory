# Native Paseo audit — 0.17.4

## Scope and verdict

Static trace of the Factory's runtime, client, and maintenance entry points against the installed 0.17.4 SDK, protocol, CLI and daemon JavaScript. At audit time `src/*.mjs` was authoritative and the plugin carried a generated copy of it (removed since; the core now lives in `plugin/` directly). The generated copy was counted only at its source. No live agents, provider calls, crashes, plugin updates or daemon mutations were used for verification. The [Paseo SDK reference](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/main/public-docs/sdk/reference.md) and [plugin reference](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/main/public-docs/plugins/reference.md) describe the public surface. Version-specific conclusions below come from the installed implementation.

**Priorities.** Switch workspace rename and agent detach to the public SDK. Replace steady-state discovery scans with one shared subscribed projection and schedule actual deadlines. Preserve reconciliation on startup and reconnect. Preserve keyed, verified execution of Factory decisions. Fix the receipt compatibility block with a real crash/recovery validation. Keep modern usage reports intact until account selection, error status, and the age of the last successful observation have been evaluated. The public `providers.listUsage()` is the legacy projection and loses information even on 0.17.4.

**Classes.** 1 fully replaceable by public Paseo · 2 substantially simplifiable via Paseo · 3 necessary thin adapter · 4 indispensable Factory decision logic · 5 historical and removable given stated conditions · 6 insufficient evidence.
